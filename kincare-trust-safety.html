How verification works

Four checks before any profile goes live.

01
Identity verification
Every childminder confirms their identity before applying to join.
02
Enhanced DBS check
A full enhanced Disclosure and Barring Service check is required, not optional.
03
Profile review
Profiles are reviewed for completeness and accuracy before publishing.
04
Ongoing reviews
Community reviews from matched families stay attached to every profile.

Looking ahead

Building toward formal regulatory registration

Beyond our current verification process, we're exploring formal registration as a UK Childminder Agency (CMA) with Ofsted — which would bring an additional layer of independent regulatory oversight to every childminder on the platform. This is part of our roadmap, not yet in place, and we'll update this page as it progresses.
