About KinCare Connect

A platform connecting African diaspora families across the UK with culturally-matched, DBS-checked childminders — built around shared language, heritage, and care philosophy, not just postcode and price.

We're currently in a pre-launch, waitlist stage, starting with Birmingham. Join the waitlist to be first to know when matching opens in your area.
