Company description

KinCare Connect is a UK platform connecting African diaspora families with culturally-matched, DBS-checked childminders — helping families find care that reflects their language, heritage, and values, and helping childminders build client relationships within their own communities. Founded by Lubna Ardo and based in Birmingham, KinCare Connect is currently in its pre-launch, waitlist stage.
