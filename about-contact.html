Our story

Built from a search I lived myself.

Why we started

A gap I couldn't scroll past.

As a Nigerian single mother navigating the UK childcare system, I faced a challenge many African families know well: finding childcare that reflected my culture, language, and parenting philosophy.

I spent weeks scrolling through generic platforms with no way to filter by language, culture, or shared values. I interviewed dozens of childminders — many wonderful — but none who shared my background or understood the nuances of raising a child in the African diaspora.

That's when it became clear: there was a gap in the market, and more importantly, a gap in the community's access to culturally-aligned childcare.

KinCare Connect exists to close that gap.

Our mission

To empower African diaspora families with culturally-matched, trustworthy childcare — and help childminders build thriving businesses within their communities.
